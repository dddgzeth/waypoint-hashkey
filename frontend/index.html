What it does for you.

The same plan engine behind every action, so they combine freely.

Move and swap

Same-chain swaps through a DEX aggregator with an automatic fallback route, and transfers to any address.

Bridge across chains

Cross-chain routes through Relay and LI.FI, including to and from X Layer, with ERC-20 approvals handled for you.

Earn on Aave

Supply, withdraw and borrow against the live best yield, read straight from the chain rather than a stale list.

Stay on it

Recurring buys and health-factor protection run in the background, using only the action you pre-authorized.

See everything

One overview of balances and DeFi positions across all your wallets and chains.

Control, stated plainly.

Two kinds of wallet, with different trust. You choose per task.

Hands-free

Execution wallets

Waypoint signs for you from wallets created for this purpose, so automations can run while you are away. They are custodial: keep working balances here, and export the key at any time.

Yours

Linked wallets

Your own wallet, verified by its signature. Waypoint builds each transaction and your wallet signs every step. Waypoint never holds these keys.

Other agents can use it too.
